Privacy Policy — Merula apps

Effective date: 30 October 2026

This policy covers the Merula apps listed below. Each app has its own section; the general rules come first.

For every Merula app

  • No account. You never sign up or sign in, except where an app's section below says otherwise.
  • No ads and no analytics. We don't show ads, and we don't track how you use the app.
  • On your phone. What the app works on stays on your phone unless an app's section below says otherwise.
  • Purchases. Optional upgrades are sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card, UPI or bank details. The app asks Google Play on your phone whether you own an upgrade and remembers the answer on the phone.
  • Google Play. Google Play may collect the standard information it collects for every installed app (such as crash and install statistics); see Google's privacy policy.
  • Children. Our apps are not directed at children under 13 and do not knowingly collect data from them.

QR & Barcode Scanner, Creator

QR & Barcode Scanner, Creator reads and creates QR codes and barcodes on your phone. Camera pictures are never saved and never leave your phone. Scans leave the phone only if you turn on "Push scans to a server" and enter a server address yourself.

What the app uses:

  • Camera. The live camera preview is used only to find and decode codes. Frames are processed in memory and discarded immediately. No photo or video is saved or sent.
  • Photos you choose. When you scan from a photo, the image you pick in Android's photo picker is read in memory to find codes and then discarded. The app gets access only to that one picture and does not keep or send it.
  • QR codes you create. What you type into the generator stays on your phone. A code image is written only when you tap Share (a temporary file handed to the app you share with) or Save (to your Pictures folder).
  • Scan history. The text of each code you scan, its type and the time are stored in the app's private storage on your phone so you can see, copy, share or export them. You can clear the history at any time; uninstalling the app deletes it.
  • Settings. Your preferences, including any server address you enter, are stored on your phone.

When data leaves your phone: only if you enable Push scans to a server in Settings and enter a host. Each scan's text, type, time, decoding details and your phone's make and model are then sent to that address, and to no other. Merula operates no server for this app and never receives your scans. Transfer to an `https://` address is encrypted; a plain `http://` address on a local network is not. Sharing a result or exporting CSV uses Android's share sheet and goes only where you choose.

Third-party components: the app uses Google ML Kit barcode scanning, which runs entirely on the phone.

Astro Panchang

Astro Panchang (Vedic astrology and panchang) collects no personal data. Everything is calculated on your phone, and your details never leave it.

What is stored on your phone: the birth details you enter (name, date, time and place of birth) for yourself and anyone you add, an optional workplace name with its founding and joining dates, the name, date, time and place of passing of anyone you add for shraddha dates, the life events you mark (their kind and date), the place you choose (or that your location gives) for the daily panchanga, and your settings (notification time, chart style, lessons finished). They are kept in the app's private storage and never leave the phone. Uninstalling the app, or deleting a person or an entry, removes them.

  • Sharing. When you tap "Share this panchanga", the app draws a picture of the day's panchanga and hands it, with a few lines of text, to the app you pick (such as WhatsApp). Nothing else is shared, and nothing is shared unless you do this. The home-screen widget is drawn on the phone.
  • Internet. None of your details are sent. Planet positions come from an astronomy library built into the app, and the list of places is stored in the app, so it works offline.
  • Location (optional). If you tap "Use my location" or turn on "Follow my location", the app reads your phone's approximate location while the app is open, only to pick the nearest town for your daily panchanga. It stores that town with coordinates rounded to about a kilometre, on the phone only. The location is never sent anywhere and never read in the background. You can turn this off in Settings or choose a town by hand.
  • Notifications. If you allow them, the app shows a morning update worked out on the phone. You can switch it off in Settings.
  • Not professional advice. Readings describe tendencies in the traditional way. They are not medical, legal, financial or psychological advice.

SVMT Seva

SVMT Seva is the seva-receipt and prasada-counter app of Sri Veerabhadra Mahammayi Devasthana, Kallapu, made by Merula for the temple. It is used by the temple's office bearers at the seva and prasada counters, who sign in with a counter PIN given by the temple. It is not meant for the public. Unlike the other Merula apps, it keeps the temple's receipts on a server.

What the app sends to the temple's server:

  • Receipts. The sevas, quantities and amounts, whether the devotee paid by cash or UPI (only that word; no card, UPI ID or bank details), the counter and the time.
  • Devotee details, only if the devotee gives them. Name, mobile number, gotra and nakshatra, entered by the office bearer for the sankalpa or for a WhatsApp receipt.
  • Prasada strike-offs. Which receipt and which items were given, at which counter and when.
  • Counter sign-in. The counter and its PIN. The server keeps PINs only in hashed form.

Where it is kept: on a cloud server in Mumbai, India, run by Merula for the temple. The app talks to it only over HTTPS, so data is encrypted in transit. The server keeps hourly backups on the same machine for 14 days. Only the temple's admin counter can see or export the receipts. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else or used for ads; there are no ads and no analytics. The server's web log records each phone's IP address and the time of each request, used only to block repeated wrong PINs, and is kept for up to 90 days.

On the phone:

  • Camera. At the prasada counter the camera reads the receipt's QR code. Pictures are processed in memory and never saved or sent.
  • Bluetooth printer. Receipts are printed on the counter's paired Bluetooth printer and go only to that printer.
  • WhatsApp receipt (optional). If a devotee asks for one, the office bearer types the devotee's number and the app opens WhatsApp with the receipt text and a picture of the slip, for the office bearer to send. The number is kept with the receipt unless the box is unticked.
  • Offline. Receipts made without a network are kept on the phone until they reach the server.

Keeping and deleting data: receipts are the temple's accounts for the festival and are kept as long as the temple needs them for its records. A devotee who wants their name or mobile number removed from a receipt can ask the temple office or write to merulallc@gmail.com, and it will be removed. Practice (training) receipts are kept on a separate practice server and cleared by the admin.
