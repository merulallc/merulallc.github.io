Privacy Policy — Carnatic Practice (Tanpura, Tala & Pitch)

Effective date: 10 October 2026
Developer: Merula
Contact: merulallc@gmail.com

Summary

Carnatic Practice collects no personal data. Your voice is analysed on your phone and is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. There is no account, no ads and no analytics.

Microphone

Pitch check, "Find my Sa", the Evaluate tab's "Sing now" and the Identify tab's listening use the microphone, only while you have them switched on and only while the app is open. The sound is analysed in memory, a fraction of a second at a time, to measure its pitch, and is then discarded. No audio is recorded, stored or transmitted. The microphone switches off when you stop listening or leave the app.

Singing evaluations and identifications

Evaluate measures a take you sing or play (voice, veena, violin, flute or mridangam) in the app, or an audio file you choose (for example a voice note). Identify recognises the Sa, raga and clapped tala of music it hears through the microphone or of an audio file you choose. The sound is analysed on your phone in the same way as Pitch check and is never stored or sent; a file you choose is only read, never copied or changed. The results — scores, practice advice, the measured numbers (such as "Sa −12¢ on average"), what Identify named (Sa, raga, tala) and a coarse pitch line for the graphs (ten values a second, from which no sound can be rebuilt) — are saved on your phone so you can follow your progress; Identify keeps its latest 30 results the same way. You can delete any evaluation or identification in the app, and uninstalling the app deletes them all. Raga and Sa recognition run entirely on your phone.

Ragas you name

In Identify you can confirm a raga ("Yes, that's right") or name the right one ("Wrong raga?"). The app then keeps that take's coarse pitch line and the raga's name on your phone, and uses them to recognise ragas better for you. If you tap "Share them as a file", the app makes a file of those pitch lines and raga names (no audio, no name or other personal details) and hands it to the app you pick, for example to send to a teacher; the app sends nothing by itself. "Undo" on a take removes its example, "Forget them" removes them all, and so does uninstalling the app.

Listen

Listen sings or plays a raga's swaras with sounds made on your phone. It uses no microphone and no data of yours.

Settings

Your Sa, tala, tempo, raga and similar preferences are stored on your phone so they are there next time. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Network

The app does not need or use the internet for any of its features. Google Play may collect the standard information it collects for every installed app (such as crash and install statistics); see Google's privacy policy.

Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card, UPI or bank details. The app asks Google Play on your phone whether you own Pro, and remembers the answer on the phone.

Children

The app can be used by music students of any age. Because it collects no personal data at all, it collects none from children.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted here with a new effective date.
