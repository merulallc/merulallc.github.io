Carnatic Practice

Tanpura, tala, pitch check, evaluation and raga identifier for daily Carnatic music practice. For voice, veena, violin, flute and mridangam.

How to use it

A four-minute guide to each tab: Tanpura and your Sa, Tala, Pitch check, Evaluate, Identify and Listen.

What it does

  • Tanpura at your own Sa, by key and kattai, or sing your Sa and let the app find it. First string Pa, Ma or Ni.
  • Tala: Adi, Rupakam, Misra Chapu, Khanda Chapu and Eka, shown as claps, counts and waves, with nadai and 1st, 2nd and 3rd speed practice.
  • Pitch check shows the swara you sing and how many cents sharp or flat you are, drawn on the raga's swara lines.
  • Evaluate scores a take for shruti, raga, steadiness and laya, and gives you a practice plan. It also works on a recording, like a WhatsApp voice note.
  • Identify names the raga, the Sa and the tala of what you sing, play or record.
  • Listen plays any raga's arohanam and avarohanam at your Sa, sung or on flute or violin, plain or with gamakas.

The tanpura, tala and Listen are free with no limits. Pitch check is free for 5 minutes a day, Evaluate for one take and Identify for three. Carnatic Practice Pro makes them unlimited.

Private by design

Your voice or instrument is analysed on the phone and never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. No account, no ads. Privacy policy.
