Privacy Policy — QR & Barcode Scanner, Creator

Effective date: 29 October 2026
Developer: Merula
Contact: merulallc@gmail.com

Summary

QR & Barcode Scanner, Creator reads and creates QR codes and barcodes on your phone. It has no account, no ads and no analytics. Camera pictures are never saved and never leave your phone. Scans leave the phone only if you turn on "Push scans to a server" and enter a server address yourself.

What the app uses

  • Camera. The live camera preview is used only to find and decode codes. Frames are processed in memory and discarded immediately. No photo or video is saved or sent.
  • Photos you choose. When you scan from a photo, the image you pick in Android's photo picker is read in memory to find codes and then discarded. The app gets access only to that one picture and does not keep or send it.
  • QR codes you create. What you type into the generator stays on your phone. A code image is written only when you tap Share (a temporary file handed to the app you share with) or Save (to your Pictures folder).
  • Scan history. The text of each code you scan, its type and the time are stored in the app's private storage on your phone so you can see, copy, share or export them. You can clear the history at any time; uninstalling the app deletes it.
  • Settings. Your preferences, including any server address you enter, are stored on your phone.

When data leaves your phone

Only if you enable Push scans to a server in Settings and enter a host. Each scan's text, type, time, decoding details and your phone's make and model are then sent to that address — and to no other. Merula operates no server for this app and never receives your scans. Transfer to an `https://` address is encrypted; a plain `http://` address on a local network is not.

Sharing a result or exporting CSV uses Android's share sheet and goes only where you choose.

Third-party components

The app uses Google ML Kit barcode scanning, which runs entirely on the phone. Google Play may collect the standard information it collects for every installed app (such as crash and install statistics); see Google's privacy policy.

Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card, UPI or bank details. The app asks Google Play on your phone whether you own Pro, and remembers the answer on the phone.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
